HUONG DAN DANG NHAP SSH MAY CHU (QUA VPN)

YEU CAU: Phai ket noi VPN truoc khi SSH.

1. Mo PowerShell hoac Windows Terminal tren may Windows.

2. Dan lenh sau va nhan Enter:

ssh iec@10.170.100.152

3. Neu lan dau ket noi co thong bao:
   Are you sure you want to continue connecting (yes/no/[fingerprint])? 
   Doi chieu fingerprint voi co/quan tri vien. Neu dung, go yes va Enter.
   Khong viet yes chung vao lenh SSH.

4. Khi hien password:, nhap mat khau da cap va nhan Enter.
   Mat khau khong hien ky tu hay dau * khi go. Day la binh thuong.
   Mat khau khong duoc luu trong tep nay; xem lai tin nhan cap tai khoan.

5. Khi dang nhap thanh cong, co the kiem tra:

whoami
hostname
pwd
nvidia-smi

6. De thoat khoi may chu:

exit

THONG TIN KET NOI
Tai khoan: iec
May chu: 10.170.100.152
Cong SSH: 22 (mac dinh)
Dieu kien: Phai bat VPN truoc

LENH SCP - TAI CHECKPOINT TU SERVER VE MAY
Tai mot file cu the:
scp iec@10.170.100.152:/duong/dan/tren/server/checkpoint.pt "c:\New folder (3)\PE-CIR\checkpoints\"

Tai ca thu muc:
scp -r iec@10.170.100.152:/duong/dan/tren/server/checkpoints/ "c:\New folder (3)\PE-CIR\checkpoints\"

LOI THUONG GAP
- Permission denied: kiem tra tai khoan va mat khau; luu y Caps Lock.
- Connection timed out: Chua bat VPN hoac kiem tra Internet.
- Connection refused: kiem tra IP va tinh trang SSH server.
- REMOTE HOST IDENTIFICATION HAS CHANGED: hoi quan tri vien xac nhan truoc khi sua known_hosts.
