HUONG DAN DANG NHAP SSH MAY CHU AIOTLAB

1. Mo PowerShell hoac Windows Terminal tren may Windows.

2. Dan lenh sau va nhan Enter:

ssh quynhptit@aiotlab.viu.edu.vn -p 30101

3. Neu lan dau ket noi co thong bao:
   Are you sure you want to continue connecting (yes/no/[fingerprint])?
   Doi chieu fingerprint voi co/quan tri vien. Neu dung, go yes va Enter.
   Khong viet yes chung vao lenh SSH.

4. Khi hien password:, nhap mat khau co da cap va nhan Enter.
   Mat khau khong hien ky tu hay dau * khi go. Day la binh thuong.
   Mat khau khong duoc luu trong tep nay; xem lai tin nhan cap tai khoan.

5. Khi dang nhap thanh cong, co the kiem tra:

whoami
hostname
pwd
nvidia-smi

6. De thoat khoi may chu:

exit

THONG TIN KET NOI
Tai khoan: quynhptit
May chu: aiotlab.viu.edu.vn
Cong SSH: 30101

LOI THUONG GAP
- Permission denied: kiem tra tai khoan va mat khau; luu y Caps Lock.
- Connection timed out: kiem tra Internet va hoi co may chu co truy cap duoc khong.
- Connection refused: kiem tra cong 30101 va tinh trang dich vu SSH voi quan tri vien.
- REMOTE HOST IDENTIFICATION HAS CHANGED: hoi quan tri vien xac nhan truoc khi sua known_hosts.

LUU Y
- Khong them dau \ truoc @ trong lenh SSH.
- Mat khau duoc nhap rieng khi SSH hoi, khong dan vao lenh.
- Thong nhat khung gio va GPU voi co truoc khi chay huan luyen.
